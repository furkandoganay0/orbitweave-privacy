Orbitweave — Privacy Policy

Orbitweave does not collect personal data through the app. No developer account, advertising, analytics or tracking is required.

On-device data

Scores, gameplay preferences and tutorial progress are stored on your device. These are not sent to us. Your device backup settings are managed by Apple.

Purchases

Optional App Store purchases are processed by Apple. The app may use Apple's StoreKit services to check purchase access and restore purchases. We do not receive your card details or operate a purchase-tracking server. Apple's privacy policy applies to its services.

Support and this website

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request information under its own privacy policy. This page does not add analytics or advertising scripts.
