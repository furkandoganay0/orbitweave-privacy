Orbitweave: Tap & Flow — Support

Two orbits, one touch. Tap the playfield to switch lanes, collect mint diamonds and avoid coral arcs.

Help

Email furkando01@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and a description of the issue. Do not send passwords or payment details.

Playing

Use How to play from the home screen for the rules. Pause during a run to take a break. Sound, haptics and reduced motion can be adjusted in Settings. Scores and preferences are saved locally on your iPhone.

Purchases and restoration

If an optional purchase is not recognized, make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account used to buy it, then use Restore purchases in the app. Contact support if access is still missing. Apple handles billing and refund requests; never send us your payment details.
